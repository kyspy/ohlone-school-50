About Ohlone

50 Years of Ohlone

As we celebrate the 50th anniversary of Ohlone, we invite you to explore this website which captures the experience of an Ohlone education through personal narratives, historical documents, and reflections from our community.

This archive brings together stories from students, parents, teachers, alumni, and community members to document how Ohlone grew from an innovative alternative school into a lasting and beloved part of Palo Alto history.

Thank you for being part of the Ohlone story.

Special thanks to the Ohlone PTA and the Palo Alto Historical Association.
