Email Summary of Lunar New Year Meeting 

Mon 1/11/10

THANK YOU TO EVERYONE WHO VOLUNTEERED TO MAKE THIS HAPPEN!

Year of the Tiger  Lunar New Year Celebration Feb 16-19 Tues - Fri 2010

Purpose: educate about the symbolism and traditions of lunar new year (celebrating the arrival of spring, new beginnings and renewal), share culture and celebrate together

Lunchtime Activities

  • TUES: creating flowering spring branches with paint and tissue paper and/or name stamps (chops) out of styrofoam (need someone to head this booth-- otherwise we will not have an activity on this day)

  • WED: Chinese calligraphy-- kids learn to write a Chinese character using traditional brush and ink on red paper. Take home and put on door for good luck.  (Yan Chen, Bibiana, Ming, and Li Yun)

  • THURS: information about Chinese zodiac signs so kids can find out what animal they are and what it means, take a little card of info home (Weldon/Xiaomei and Bibiana)

  • FRI: making paper lanterns (Nikki Pham-Chandra),  ribbon sticks (Diana Lee Rm22 and Rm2), and maraca/tamborines (Sue Correll Rm 26) for parade

Ask students to bring in a spring flowering branch such as plum, apple, apricot, cherry, quince, etc. put them in vases in the office and classrooms as heralds of spring. WED 2/17
