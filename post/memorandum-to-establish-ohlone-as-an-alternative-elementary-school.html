Memorandum to Establish Ohlone as an Alternative Elementary School

MEMORANDUM 

February 26, 1976

To: Board of Education 

From: Newman Walker, Superintendent 

Subject: Recognition of Ohlone as District Alternative School

 

Having followed the Board approved procedures for determining alternative status for Ohlone Elementary School, I wish to inform you that Ohlone has met all the criteria established by the Board in its policy regarding alternative elementary schools. 

At the close of the registration period on February 20, 1976, it was determined that the majority of the enrollees will come from outside the Ohlone attendance 

area. 

Therefore, I recommend that the Board recognize Ohlone as a district alternative elementary school and that the former Ohlone attendance area become part of the Fairmeadow attendance area.
